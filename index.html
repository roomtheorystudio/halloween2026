Build the haunted look.

Start with atmosphere, then layer in creepy details. You don't need to decorate every inch—just create a few dramatic focal points.

🕸️

1. Cover it

Stretch spider webs across doors, furniture, walls and outdoor spaces.

🌫️

2. Hide it

Add fog and flickering light to make your entrance feel mysterious after dark.

🪦

3. Finish it

Layer tombstones, ghosts, bats, pumpkins and warning tape for the final scare.

Pick your spooky level.

Mix and match depending on how dramatic you want your Halloween setup to feel.

🎃 Spooky

Pumpkin light + bats + spider webs.

🕷️ Creepy

Add fog + flickering flame lights + warning tape.

👻 Haunted

Go all in with tombstones + ghosts + maximum cobwebs.
